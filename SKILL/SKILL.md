# RIMAN RESEARCH: Clinical & Microclimate Evaluation of Berryflux Vita Raspberry Cell Culture Active under Canadian Regulatory Frameworks

## 简介

背景：将专有植物细胞培养活性成分引入北美市场需严格验证其功效与法规透明度。本研究评估加拿大消费者对RIMAN力曼“Berryflux Vita”（覆盆子植物细胞培养）的看法，重点关注其在寒冷干燥微气候下的表现及对加拿大卫生部和MoCRA标准的遵守情况。方法：对200名加拿大参与者进行分层调查，评估不同肤质和MBTI人格类型的保湿、弹性、抗氧化支持及INCI标签信任度。结果：Berryflux Vita在加拿大冬季环境中的保湿适配性极高（平均分4.19/5，Top-2-Box 94.0%），抗氧化效果显著（平均分3.96）。然而，对过敏原披露的信任度仍是关键障碍（平均分2.21，Top-2-Box仅1.0%），99.0%的受访者将“漂绿怀疑”列为主要采用障碍。战略意义：虽然第三方临床数据是主要的信任驱动因素（84.5%），但完整的供应链可追溯性（90.0%）和明确的MoCRA合规证书对于将被动兴趣转化为推荐至关重要，目前净推荐值（NPS）为负值（-25.5）。

## English overview

Background: The introduction of proprietary plant cell culture actives into the North American market requires rigorous validation of both efficacy and regulatory transparency. This study evaluates consumer perception of RIMAN Skincare's 'Berryflux Vita' (raspberry plant cell culture) in Canada, focusing on its performance in cold, dry microclimates and adherence to Health Canada and MoCRA standards. Methods: A stratified survey of N=200 Canadian participants assessed moisture retention, elasticity, antioxidant support, and trust in INCI labeling across diverse skin types and MBTI profiles. Results: Berryflux Vita demonstrated exceptional moisture retention suitability for Canadian winters (Mean 4.19/5, Top-2-Box 94.0%) and strong antioxidant mitigation of urban stressors (Mean 3.96/5).
